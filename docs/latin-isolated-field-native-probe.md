# Native probe of isolated source grammar fields

The eight-case extraction-blocker review contains five source-field isolation
trials. Two emit definitions under the original literal lemma. The new probe
recreates all five trials against their original source occurrences, verifies
the qualified private receipt and every transcript, and counts successful
trials separately from unique affected lemmas and unique lost signatures.

Each successful trial receives its own private native assembly. The entire
final candidate is preserved byte for byte and only the isolated trial's
definitions are appended, under an absent literal lemma. No two source fields
are combined. The baseline's three retained verbal inputs and the controlled
nominal indexes must match the candidate before building a copy. New verbal
indexes are built with the existing native tools. The production and final
candidate roots are never modified.

For each trial the probe measures the source headword as a direct first-person
singular present indicative, with active or passive morphology as written.
The old and new headword readings are also compared with the eleven grammar
fields plus five native decomposition fields. All text fields are checked
against truncation. A missing expected headword reading is a measured failure,
not permission to silently discard the trial.

The qualified lost forms for that trial's lemma are independently reprobed
against the baseline and must reproduce the exact target multiset. They must
remain complete losses in the final candidate. Trial output is split into
exact target recoveries, still-missing readings and other readings, with tense
and grammar/provenance profiles. Recognition alone does not establish correct
analyses. These are per-trial measurements; they cannot be summed as unique
recoveries when two trials belong to one lemma or share a target form.

A second assembly isolates the unique literal present `:vs:` from the full
trial expansion and omits all derivative, past-stem and word directives. It
must expand to exactly one present class. This independent build repeats the
headword and exact loss controls and is compared directly to the full trial
on the target forms. The comparison distinguishes recoveries gained or lost
and productive output removed by isolating the present stem. A derivative can
remain productive even if its explicit expansion shows only a present class.

The source-field choice remains a diagnostic counterfactual. Successful native
output does not justify discarding the other source fields, reconstructing
past parts or approving the complete paradigm. No present family is inferred
from a newly expanded stem, and no global regression comparison is claimed.

Headers, literal definitions, target forms and detailed native decompositions
remain in exclusive mode-0600 dossiers under mode-0700 trial directories
outside the repository. The public report contains only counts, fixed grammar
categories and hashes. Nothing private is uploaded as a CI artifact. The four
final candidate index hashes are checked again at completion. Results and
the next source-arbitration steps are recorded in PR #18.
